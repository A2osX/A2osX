				bcs EXP.Eval.ESYN

				jsr EXP.StackPushV		make sure on stack
				bcs EXP.Eval.Err

.33				plx						get OP(n-1)
				bmi .38					$ff....

				bcc .33

				bcs EXP.Eval.Err

.38				phx						push back OP(n-1)

				lda ZPPtr3				get OP(n)
				pha						push OP(n) on stack

				jmp .10					go check for next token
*--------------------------------------
